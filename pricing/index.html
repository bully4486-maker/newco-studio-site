Questions people ask

How much does a one-page website cost in the UK?

At NewCo Studio a one-page website costs £99, fixed in writing before work starts. It includes your services, prices, photos and contact details on one mobile-friendly page, basic SEO, and every file handed over. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

How much does it cost to fix a website?

Small website fixes at NewCo Studio start from £19, for example a broken link, wrong contact details, a button or form that doesn't work, or a layout problem on phones. You get the exact price in writing first. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

When do I pay?

You get a fixed written quote first. Then it's 50% to start and 50% after you've signed off the finished work, both by UK bank transfer. A £19 fix is paid in one go once you've approved it.

Are there any monthly fees?

No. NewCo Studio doesn't charge a subscription. You pay the fixed price for the job, and you own the site and its files.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

Yes. NewCo Studio works remotely with small businesses across the UK and worldwide. Prices are quoted in pounds sterling (GBP).
