What the example sites are showing you.

Open these on your phone. Each note is about a pattern: the hero, the prices, the button, and the menu. The pictures are the top of each page, cropped the way a phone crops a tall screen.

These are examples of our work. We designed and built them to show the standard a small-business site can meet. They are not a client list, and this is not a logo wall. We don't show reviews or logos we don't have.

Café example

Hearth & Crumb

A café and bakery page: one promise, a menu you can scan, and a table booking that is easy to find.

Hero
A full-bleed photo, one line about slow mornings and proper coffee, then two actions: book a table, or see the menu.
Prices
Menu items sit on the page with a price beside each one, and a full menu page carries the rest.
Button
Book a table is in the header and again at the end of the page. One next step, repeated.
Mobile nav
On a narrow screen the logo and links wrap onto more than one line. The booking button stays a button, not a buried link.

Trades example

Halvercroft Electrical

A trades page that says what the job is, shows starting prices, and asks for a written quote.

Hero
A photograph, a plain-English promise, then Get a quote and See prices. The visitor does not have to hunt for either.
Prices
“From” prices are on the page for typical jobs, with a separate price list for the longer set. The quote still fixes the real total.
Button
Get a quote is in the header and again as a form at the end of the page.
Mobile nav
Services, the price list and the quote stay reachable. The links tighten instead of disappearing.

Groomer example

Paws Parlour

A grooming page with calm copy, prices by size and coat, and booking that stays in reach on a phone.

Hero
A photo of the groom, a short promise, and Book a groom. The second action is the price list, not a second competing offer.
Prices
Services show a “from” price on the page. A prices page breaks the same work down by size and coat.
Button
Book a groom is in the header, in the hero, and as a bar that stays on screen on a small phone.
Mobile nav
Services, prices and booking stay on screen. The sticky bar repeats the booking action so it is not lost under the menu.
