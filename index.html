How it works

Four steps, all in writing.

  1. Written brief

    Fill in the quote form (or email keith@newcopacks.co.uk) with what you need, examples and your deadline. A few lines is fine.

  2. Fixed quote

    We reply with the scope, the price, the delivery date and how many revisions are included.

  3. Build & delivery

    We build it and send a preview link or files to check. Progress updates stay in the thread.

  4. Revisions & hand-over

    You see your page and we make the agreed revisions. Once you sign it off, you pay the 50% balance by bank transfer and we hand over every file.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

How much does a one-page website cost in the UK?

At NewCo Studio a one-page website for a small business costs £99, fixed in writing before any work starts. That covers your services, prices, photos and contact details on one page that works properly on phones, with basic SEO and every file handed over.

Where can I get a cheap website for my small business in the UK?

NewCo Studio builds one-page websites for small businesses for £99. You get a written fixed quote within 24 hours, you see the page before you pay the balance, and you own the site. It works remotely with businesses anywhere in the UK and worldwide.

Can you fix a broken link or a small problem on my website?

Yes. Website fixes start from £19: broken links, a wrong phone number or email, a button or form that doesn't work, or a page that looks wrong on phones. Tell us what's wrong in the quote form and you get a fixed price in writing first.

Do you only work with businesses near you?

No. NewCo Studio is UK-based and works remotely, by email and the website quote form, with small businesses anywhere in the UK and worldwide. Prices are in pounds sterling (GBP).

Why written-only? Can we have a call?

We work in writing, by email or through the quote form on our website. You get a clear record of what was agreed, you can reply when it suits you, and nothing gets lost between a call and the build. Most small jobs are quicker to scope in a few messages than a meeting.

How do I pay?

You get a fixed written quote first. Once you're happy, we send an invoice: 50% to start and 50% when you've approved the finished site, paid by UK bank transfer. You only pay the balance once you've signed it off, and everything is handed over to you once it's paid. Small fixes (£19) are paid in one go by bank transfer once you've approved the fix.

How long does a job take?

Small fixes are often a day or two. A one-page site is usually a few days once we have your text and photos. Your quote gives a firm delivery date before you commit.

What do I need to send?

What the job is, any logo, text, photos or files, a couple of sites or examples you like, and your deadline. If something's missing, we'll ask in the thread.

Who owns the website or code?

You do. You get the full source files when the job is done. Your domain and hosting stay in your name.

Are the example sites client work?

No. They're example sites we designed and built ourselves to show the standard of our work. We don't show reviews or client logos we don't have.

How fast will I hear back?

You get a written fixed quote within 24 hours of sending the form, usually sooner. If the job isn't a fit, we'll tell you that within 24 hours too.

Can I request edits after delivery?

Yes. Further edits after delivery are quoted as their own fixed-price jobs. A small change is priced like any other fix, from £19, and you get the price in writing before we start.

Do you do hosting or ongoing maintenance?

We don't sell hosting. We'll help you put the site on a host in your own name, and later changes can be quoted as small fixed-price jobs.

Got a small job that needs doing well?

Send a short brief. You'll get a fixed price in writing within 24 hours, before any work starts.
