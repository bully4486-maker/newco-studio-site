Usually a few days once we have your text and photos. Your written quote gives a firm delivery date before you commit. Need it a day sooner? That's an add-on from £26.

You get a fixed written quote first. Then it's 50% to start and 50% after you've signed off the finished site, both by UK bank transfer. You see your page before you pay the balance, and every file is handed over once it's paid.
