FAQ

Quick answers.

Is a one-page website enough?

A one-page website for £99 is enough when the whole offer fits on one page: what you do, your prices, your photos and how to get in touch. If you need Home, Services, About, Contact and one more page, that is the £299 fixed website of up to 5 pages. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

What is the difference between £99 and £299 fixed?

£99 is one page, a few days once we have text and photos. £299 fixed is up to 5 pages, with 1 revision round, a preview by working day 3 and live by working day 5 after the deposit clears. You pay half to start and half on sign-off, by UK bank transfer. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

How do I pay?

You get a fixed written quote first. For a £299 fixed website or a £99 one-page website, you pay half to start and half on sign-off, by UK bank transfer. You see the work before the second half. A fix from £19 is paid once you have approved it. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

Do you work outside my area?

The work is done remotely, UK-wide and online only. NewCo Studio is a UK studio run by Keith Bullock. It works with small businesses across the UK by email and the quote form. There is no office and no meeting in person. Prices are in pounds sterling. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

Are the example sites real customer websites?

No. They are example sites we designed and built ourselves to show the standard of our work. We don't show reviews or client logos we don't have. Open the pages and judge them on your own phone. They are not a list of customer businesses. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

Do I need to phone you?

No. A phone call is not needed. We work in writing, by email or through the quote form, so the price and the scope stay on record. You can reply when it suits you. Most small jobs are clearer in a few written messages. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.
