FAQ

How much does a small business website cost in the UK?

At NewCo Studio a small business website of up to 5 pages is £299 fixed. The pages are Home, Services, About, Contact, and one of Gallery, FAQ or Pricing. A one-page website is £99. Website fixes are from £19. The price is agreed in writing before any work starts. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

How long does a £299 fixed website take?

A £299 fixed website has a preview by working day 3 and is live by working day 5 after the deposit clears. The clock starts once that first payment has cleared. A one-page site usually takes a few days once we have your text and photos. Your quote names the date. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

What is included in £299 fixed?

£299 fixed includes up to 5 pages: Home, Services, About, Contact, and one of Gallery, FAQ or Pricing. Copy is written from your answers. You get a mobile-first layout, basic SEO, an enquiry form and 1 revision round. You own the files. A domain name is not included. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

How do I pay?

You get a fixed written quote first. For a £299 fixed website or a £99 one-page website, you pay half to start and half on sign-off, by UK bank transfer. You see the work before the second half. A fix from £19 is paid once you have approved it. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

You buy the domain yourself, in your own name, from any registrar. A domain is not included in £299 fixed or in the £99 page. Hosting stays in your name too. We can put the site on your Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages account, or send a zip. We don't sell hosting. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

What is the difference between £99 and £299 fixed?

£99 is one page, a few days once we have text and photos. £299 fixed is up to 5 pages, with 1 revision round, a preview by working day 3 and live by working day 5 after the deposit clears. You pay half to start and half on sign-off, by UK bank transfer. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

Do you meet in person?

No. Everything is online. The work is done remotely, UK-wide and online only. You send the quote form or an email, get a written price, and review the page on a link. There is no office and we do not meet in person. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.
