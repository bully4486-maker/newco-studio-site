What's included

The whole job is one clear page.

A one-page website is the right size when a new customer needs the facts in one place. A café can show the offer and the prices. A tradesperson can list the work and the towns they cover. A market stall can say which day it is there, and how to buy on the other days. Anything that needs a catalogue, a checkout or staff logins is a different job, and the quote will say so.

  • Your offer, in your wordsServices or products, with prices or a clear “from” price. We don't invent a menu you don't have.
  • Photos you already haveThe shop, the work, the product. We don't dress the page with pictures of someone else's premises.
  • A way to make contactPhone, email, a map link, or a short note. A booking system is only included if the written quote says so.
  • Readable on a phoneType, buttons and the contact path are built for a small screen first.
  • Basic search and sharingA title, a meta description, a favicon, and an image for when the link is pasted into a message.
  • Files handed overYou receive the page files. The domain and the hosting account stay in your name.

What needs its own quote

Say it in the form if any of these are the actual job. You will get a fixed price for that scope, or a clear decline if we shouldn't take it.

  • Several pages, a shop with card payments, or a diary that takes bookings on its own.
  • Hosting sold by us, or a monthly care plan. We don't sell either. We help you put the files on a host in your name, including hosts that are free for a simple page, so the site is still yours if you stop writing to us.
  • A promised position on Google. The page gets a title, a description and text that match the business. We don't sell rankings.
  • Copy, reviews or a client list we don't have. The example sites were designed by us to show the standard. They are not a portfolio of local customers.

A page you already have, with one thing wrong, is usually a website fix from £19, not a new build. A dead link, an old phone number, a form that doesn't send, or a layout that falls apart on a phone sits in that bracket when the change is small. You pay for a fix once you have approved it.

How it runs

Remote from the first message.

There is no studio to visit. The record of the job is the writing: the form, the quote, and your sign-off.

  1. Tell us the job

    Use the one-page quote form: your name, how to reply, and the type of business. One sentence on what the page must do is enough to start.

  2. A fixed quote

    Within 24 hours you get the price, what is included, how many revision rounds, and a delivery date. Work starts when you accept that in writing.

  3. You send the material

    Text, logo, photos, and the places you cover. If something is missing, we ask in the same thread. We don't fill the gap with invented detail.

  4. Preview, then the balance

    50% to start and 50% after you sign the page off, by UK bank transfer. You see the page before the balance. Then every file is yours.

Delivery is a preview link and the files, not a parcel, and there is no shipping charge. The published window is one to five days once we have your text and photos. Your quote names the date. Later edits are new jobs from £19. Delivery and returns. Full prices are on the pricing page.

FAQ

Before you send the form.

Is £99 the price I will actually pay?

For the one-page job described on this page, yes: £99, confirmed in writing before any work starts. If you need more than that one page, the quote says so and names a different fixed price, or we decline the job. Prices as stated. VAT is not charged.

Do I have to meet you, or visit a studio?

No. NewCo Studio has no public premises. You send the brief through the quote form, we reply in writing within 24 hours, and you review the page on a link. There is no call-out and no meeting.

What should I send with the brief?

Your name, a way to reply, what the business does, and any text, logo or photos you already have. If a photo or a price is missing, we ask in the same thread rather than invent it.

Can you host the site and charge me monthly?

We don't sell hosting and we don't charge a subscription. We help you put the files on a host in your own name. The domain stays in your name too.

When do I pay for a one-page website?

After you accept the written quote: 50% to start and 50% after you sign the page off, both by UK bank transfer. You see the page before you pay the balance.
