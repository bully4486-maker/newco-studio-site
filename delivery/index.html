Delivery and returns

Delivery and returns

Last updated 6 November 2026

NewCo Studio is a UK-based web studio working remotely with clients across the UK and worldwide. This page is how the work reaches you. Prices on studio website and fix work are as stated. VAT is not charged. See pricing.

Website and fix work

Website and fix work is not a parcel. You receive files and a preview link. There is no shipping charge, in the UK or worldwide, and there is no time in the post.

You pay half to start and half on sign-off, by UK bank transfer. You see the work and sign it off before the second half. A website fix from £19 is paid in one go after you approve it. Both are paid by UK bank transfer.

Small fixes are often a day or two. A small-business website for £299 fixed is previewed by working day 3 and live by working day 5 after the deposit clears. A one-page website for £99 is usually a few days once we have your text and photos. For those two, the delivery window we publish is one to five days. Your written quote names the delivery date before you commit, and that date applies if it is different.

Finished website and fix work is not returned. There is nothing physical to send back. Further edits after delivery are quoted as new fixed-price jobs from £19, rather than as a return of the original job.

Spreadsheets on Etsy

The spreadsheets on our spreadsheets page are instant digital downloads sold on Etsy. There is no physical shipping from NewCo Studio and no shipping charge from us. You download the file from Etsy when the order is complete.

The prices on our spreadsheets page are the ones shown on Etsy's UK listing pages, including UK VAT. Etsy prices may vary by buyer location. That VAT is Etsy's charge, not the studio's.

Any refund for those downloads is handled by Etsy. This page does not set Etsy's refund terms.

Printable record books on Payhip

The printable record books and the Self Assessment guide on our spreadsheets page are PDF downloads sold through our Payhip store. There is no physical shipping. Payhip sends the download link straight after payment.

Prices on Payhip include VAT where it applies. Payhip collects and pays that VAT as the reseller of the download.

At checkout you're asked to agree that the download starts straight away. Once you've agreed and the download has started, the order can't be cancelled or returned. If a file is faulty or not as described, message us and we'll fix it, replace it or refund you. This doesn't affect your statutory rights.
