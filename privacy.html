Privacy notice

Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to personal data when you use this website. The site is run by NewCo Studio, a UK-based web studio run by Keith Bullock, which is the data controller for quote requests sent through it and for the business outreach described below.

The quote request form

If you send a quote request we receive your name, an email address, a phone number, or both (one is enough), and what you tell us about the job. On the website fixes page that can include a page address, notes about the screen size, and an optional screenshot. We use them only to reply to your request, write your quote and, if you go ahead, deliver the work. The small-business website form at /website-299/ asks for your name, email address, business name, what the business does, the services to list, the area you serve, how the writing should sound, the words for the home, about and contact pages, notes for the fifth page, how you will send photos, your domain, and whether the files should go on your Cloudflare Pages account, your GitHub Pages account, or in a zip. If you tick the optional box, we also record that you asked us to start within the 14-day cancellation period. That form does not ask for a phone number or a home address. Work does not start when you send it. Work starts once the first payment has cleared. The lawful basis is the same: taking steps at your request before a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We don't add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone else for marketing. Please don't include passwords or payment details in the form or in a screenshot.

Form submissions are passed to us by email through Web3Forms, a form-forwarding service, and stored in our email account. Please don't include sensitive information such as passwords or payment details in the form. If we have visit notes, described below, they are included in that same email.

Where a quote request came from

On the studio pages, if you send the quote form, a short script can copy a few details from the address of that page into the form. It does not run on the example sites under /demos/. Nothing is written to cookies, session storage or local storage.

If the page address includes campaign details (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign or utm_content), those values are trimmed to at most 100 characters and included. The form also includes the path of that page, such as / or /websites-99/, and, if you followed a link from another website, that site's origin, such as https://www.example.com, not the full page address. A link from this same site is not included.

Those details are sent only if you submit the quote form. Web3Forms emails them to us with the rest of your request. If you do not send the form, we never receive them. If the page address has no campaign details, the form is sent without them.

After you send the form, those notes are part of the quote email. We keep that email for the same length of time as the rest of the request, set out under How long we keep it.

Questions about these notes, or about any personal data we hold: email keith@newcopacks.co.uk.

Messages on Facebook Messenger and Instagram

If you message NewCo Studio on Facebook Messenger or Instagram, for example after tapping Send Message on one of our ads, we receive your name and profile as shown on that platform and what you write to us. We read and answer every message individually; replies may be prepared with software assistance. The only instant automated reply is Meta's greeting. We use your messages only to reply and write your quote and, if you go ahead, to deliver the work. Our lawful basis is the same as for the quote form. We don't add you to a mailing list or use your messages for marketing. Meta also processes these messages under its own terms; see Meta's Privacy Policy. We keep messages for the same length of time as form requests, set out under How long we keep it.

Business outreach

We may email limited companies and limited liability partnerships (LLPs) about their website, for example to point out a fault we noticed and offer to fix it, or to describe our small-business website at £299 fixed (up to 5 pages, live in 5 working days). We find these businesses on Companies House and on the business's own public website.

For each business we keep the company name, the company number, a business contact email address or other contact route published by the business, the website address, the fault we noticed, and the date we checked it.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): offering relevant website services to other businesses. We don't email sole traders, ordinary partnerships or individuals for marketing.

We keep these details for 12 months from our last contact with the business, then delete them. The only exception is the stop list described below.

To opt out, reply “stop” to any email from us, or email keith@newcopacks.co.uk. We then add the business's name and contact address to a suppression list, which we keep only to make sure we never contact you again.

You have the same rights over this data as over any other personal data we hold, set out under Your rights below: you can ask what we hold, and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also object to this outreach at any time, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

How long we keep it

If you don't go ahead, we delete your request, or delete our copy of your messages, within 12 months. If you hire us, we keep project correspondence for as long as needed for the work and for legal and accounting records (normally six years).

The chat helper

The “Common questions” helper is a set of pre-written answers that runs entirely in your browser. It doesn't record what you click and sends nothing to us or anyone else.

Cookies and measurement

We do not set cookies on the studio pages, and we do not use session storage or local storage. Campaign details on a quote request are copied from the current page address at the moment you send the form (see Where a quote request came from). Typefaces and images on the studio pages are served from this site. The example sites also load typefaces from Google Fonts, and their address section can load a map from OpenStreetMap. Those are requests to those services. We don't use them for advertising.

For aggregate visit counts we use a cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics script (see Site statistics below).

Site statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a free cookieless service from Cloudflare, Inc., to collect aggregate visit statistics (for example page views and roughly how many people use the site). It does not set cookies, does not build personal profiles and is not used for advertising. We rely on the PECR exception for storage and access that is solely for statistics about how a service is used. If you prefer we stop counting visits from you, email keith@newcopacks.co.uk and we will exclude your traffic where the tool allows, or remove the script.

Hosting logs

The hosting provider (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.) may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it, by replying to any email from us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
