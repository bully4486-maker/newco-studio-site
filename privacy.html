Privacy notice

Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to personal data when you use this website. The site is run by NewCo Studio, a small web studio based in Oxfordshire, UK, which is the data controller for quote requests sent through it.

The quote request form

If you send a quote request we receive your name, email address, project type, description, budget range, deadline and any file link you choose to include. We use them only to reply to your request, write your quote and, if you go ahead, deliver the work. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We don't add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone else for marketing.

Form submissions are passed to us by email through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service, and stored in our email account. Please don't include sensitive information such as passwords or payment details in the form. If we have visit notes, described below, they are included in that same email.

Where a quote request came from

On the studio pages (the home page, this page, the thank-you page and the page-not-found page), a short script can keep a few notes about your visit. It does not run on the sample demo sites. It uses your browser's session storage. That is not a cookie. We do not set a cookie for these notes, and there is no advertising pixel.

We store a note only when we actually have it:

  • Campaign details from the link you opened, when the address includes them: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign and utm_content. Each value is trimmed, and we keep at most 100 characters. If you later open another studio page in the same tab whose address includes a new value, that value replaces the earlier one.
  • The path of the first studio page in this tab, such as / or /privacy.html. We do not store the query string (anything after a ?).
  • The site you came from, if you followed a link from another website. We store only the origin, such as https://www.example.com, not the full page address. A link from this same site is not stored.

We keep these notes so that, if you ask for a quote, we can see which link and which first page the request came from. They stay in this browser tab. They are not sent to us by themselves. If your browser blocks session storage, the quote form is sent without them.

They are sent only if you submit the quote form. The form then includes them with the rest of your request, and FormSubmit emails that to us. If you do not send the form, we never receive them. They normally clear when you close the tab. Some browsers can bring them back if you restore a previous session. You can also clear them by removing site data for studio.newcopacks.co.uk in your browser.

After you send the form, those notes are part of the quote email. We keep that email for the same length of time as the rest of the request, set out under How long we keep it.

Questions about these notes, or about any personal data we hold: email keith@newcopacks.co.uk.

How long we keep it

If you don't go ahead, we delete your request within 12 months. If you hire us, we keep project correspondence for as long as needed for the work and for legal and accounting records (normally six years).

The chat helper

The “Questions?” helper is a set of pre-written answers that runs entirely in your browser. It doesn't record what you click and sends nothing to us or anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

There are no advertising cookies, no advertising pixel and no personal profiling. Visit notes for the quote form are kept in session storage in your browser, not in a cookie (see Where a quote request came from). Typefaces and images on the studio pages are served from this site. The sample demo pages also load typefaces from Google Fonts, and their address section can load a map from OpenStreetMap. Those are requests to those services. We don't use them for advertising. For aggregate visit counts we use a cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics script (see Site statistics below).

Site statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a free cookieless service from Cloudflare, Inc., to collect aggregate visit statistics (for example page views and roughly how many people use the site). It does not set cookies, does not build personal profiles and is not used for advertising. We rely on the PECR exception for storage and access that is solely for statistics about how a service is used. If you prefer we stop counting visits from you, email keith@newcopacks.co.uk and we will exclude your traffic where the tool allows, or remove the script.

Hosting logs

The hosting provider (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.) may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Hiring through Upwork

If you contact or hire us on Upwork, Upwork's own privacy policy also applies.

Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it, by replying to any email from us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
